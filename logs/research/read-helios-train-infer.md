## 1. Stage-1 training-step anatomy

### 1.1 What enters a normal Stage-1/Stage-2 step

- **OBSERVED — offline/pre-encoded inputs.** The Stage-1 dataset loads one `.pt` file and reads its already encoded `vae_latent`; it does not invoke a VAE in `__getitem__` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:324-350`). It also chooses a stored prompt embedding (including an optional per-event embedding) and returns `x0_latents`, `history_latents`, `target_latents`, and `prompt_embeds` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:365-379`). The trainer correspondingly moves those tensors to device and calls `prepare_stage1_clean_input_from_latents`; the hot path does not VAE-encode the video (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1343-1373`).
- **OBSERVED — one target chunk plus ground-truth history.** For each source video, `prepare_stage1_latent` flattens all latent sections into one time axis, prepends 19 zero history frames, deterministically samples one target-section index, slices the preceding 19 source latents as history, and slices exactly one source section as the target (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:136-172`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:188-196`). The first latent frame of the full video is separately copied as `x0_latent`, except that the anchor is zeroed when the sampled target is section 0 (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:139-174`).
- **INFERRED — teacher forcing.** This is teacher forcing at the section boundary: the model predicts one noisy target chunk while conditioning on pre-encoded ground-truth previous-video latents rather than on its own prior generated chunks. That inference follows directly from the dataset slices above and the transformer call receiving the sliced history tensors beside the noisy target (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:45-61`). There is no Stage-1 autoregressive section loop in the trainer; `_flow_loss` performs one transformer call per sampled noise/pyramid item, not a generated-history rollout (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:43-61`).
- **OBSERVED — target/noise objective.** Stage 1 samples one flow-matching timestep per batch item, constructs `x_t=(1-sigma)*x_0+sigma*noise`, and trains against `noise-x_0` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:756-802`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:881-888`). `_flow_loss` computes a weighted MSE and calls `accelerator.backward` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:63-107`), after which the trainer steps the optimizer and LR scheduler (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1484-1513`).

### 1.2 Three-tier history construction

- **OBSERVED.** `history_sizes` defaults to `[16,2,1]`, while the target `latent_window_size` defaults to `[9]` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/train_config.py:229-236`). `prepare_stage1_clean_input_from_latents` sorts the history sizes descending, verifies 19 history + 9 target latent frames, assigns absolute indices, splits the history into long=16, mid=2, short-detail=1, and prepends the separate first-frame anchor to the short tier (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:646-667`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:727-741`). The resulting ordinary shapes are long 16, mid 2, and short 2 because short contains `[anchor, one recent latent]` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1351-1359`).

### 1.3 `is_random_drop`: T2V/I2V/V2V task mixing

- **OBSERVED — T2V.** With `is_random_drop`, T2V is tested first; on a hit it zeros the first-frame anchor and all three history tiers, leaving text as the only conditioning source (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:669-689`).
- **OBSERVED — I2V.** If T2V does not trigger, I2V is tested next. It requests `hist_seq_len-1` dropped frames and zeros from the oldest long tier through mid and then short, so one most-recent history latent remains while the separately stored first-frame anchor is retained (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:690-725`). This constructs the training format described by `correct.yaml` as first-frame anchor plus last-frame history (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/scripts/training/configs/correct.yaml:8-15`).
- **OBSERVED — V2V.** If I2V does not trigger, V2V samples a number of whole 9-latent windows (plus the history remainder) to drop and zeros that many oldest long→mid→short frames, thereby retaining a variable recent history suffix (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:704-725`). If none of T2V/I2V/V2V triggers, all ground-truth history remains (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:669-730`).
- **OBSERVED — probabilities are sequential, not one normalized categorical draw.** T2V, then I2V, then V2V each use a fresh random draw in an `if/else/elif` chain (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:669-704`). Therefore the configured values are conditional branch-hit probabilities rather than three normalized mixture weights; for example, I2V is only eligible after T2V misses (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:669-704`).

### 1.4 `corrupt_history`, frame-aware corruption, blur, and saturation

- **OBSERVED — application point.** Stage-1 history corruption occurs after timestep/noise preparation (and after optional error injection) but before target/model-input corruption and before the final flow interpolation; only history tiers are passed to `corrupt_history_latents` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:795-846`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:865-883`). Stage 2 calls the same history-corruption function after constructing all pyramid target/noise pairs (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:1048-1090`).
- **OBSERVED — modes.** `corrupt_mode_history` supports `noise`, `downsample`, or `random`; `random` selects additive-noise versus downsample/upsample blur with `corrupt_mode_prob_history`, while a separate clean-probability check can return the history untouched (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/train_config.py:272-287`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:303-321`). Blur chooses a spatial scale in the configured interval, bilinearly downsamples, and bilinearly returns to the original resolution (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:188-213`). Noise linearly mixes each selected latent block with Gaussian noise using a uniformly sampled sigma up to the tier-specific maximum (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:216-226`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:362-463`).
- **OBSERVED — frame-aware semantics.** When `is_frame_independent_corrupt_history=True`, sigma has shape `[batch,1,num_frames,1,1]`, so each latent frame in a selected block receives its own strength; otherwise sigma is one scalar per batch item for that selected block (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:216-226`). Corruption reorganizes history into 9-latent windows, skips all-zero windows, corrupts nonzero windows, then restores the long/mid/short layout (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:328-399`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:401-484`).
- **OBSERVED — an important absence.** Although `is_chunk_independent_corrupt_history` is exposed in the schema and is mutually exclusive with frame-independent corruption, `corrupt_history_latents` never branches on it after the assertion and never passes it to `get_corrupt_noise_sigma`; therefore this flag does not implement a distinct chunk-independent sampling mode in the inspected code (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/train_config.py:279-287`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:283-325`).
- **OBSERVED — saturation.** The branch adds saturation after history corruption in Stage-1/2 flow training (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:848-863`). It scales channel deviations around each latent's channel mean by a random factor below or above one, preserving the first-frame anchor but applying independent clean decisions to history blocks (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:487-508`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:517-599`). `correct.yaml` is only a partial override that asks for random noise/blur plus saturation and I2V drop; it must be merged with a full stage config rather than used as a standalone complete trainer config (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/scripts/training/configs/correct.yaml:1-27`).
- **OBSERVED — inference's separately named “Frame-Aware Corruption.”** The native training-validation pipeline optionally tracks per-channel chunk mean/variance with EMA thresholds (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_base.py:694-750`). On detected drift it applies `history_latents + strength*randn_like(history_latents)` to the just-generated current section before that section is appended to the rollout history (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_base.py:752-761`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/pipelines/pipeline_helios.py:1432-1453`). Despite the log label, this inference helper does not compute per-frame adaptive strengths; it adds elementwise iid Gaussian noise at one global configured strength (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_base.py:752-761`).

## 2. Sequence geometry and what multi-section unroll would require

### 2.1 One section's geometry

- **OBSERVED.** A target section contains 9 latent frames (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/train_config.py:231-232`). The inference pipeline maps this to `(9-1)*4+1=33` decoded RGB frames using the VAE temporal scale (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1106-1110`). The conditioning slice is 19 historical latent frames partitioned as long 16, mid 2, near 1, plus a separately repeated first-frame anchor in the short input (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:142-156`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:646-667`).

### 2.2 Exactly one normal Stage-1 target, not a multi-section sample

- **OBSERVED.** `choice_idx` selects one section, and the ordinary returned `target_latent` spans only `latent_window_size` frames (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:169-196`). The trainer consumes only `history_latents`, `target_latents`, and `x0_latents`, then clears them after forming one model input (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1343-1379`). `_flow_loss` has no section dimension or history-update loop (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_base.py:20-61`).
- **OBSERVED — optional latent range is currently dead for training.** The dataset can additionally return a contiguous slice containing 19 history frames plus `num_rollout_sections` target sections when `return_all_vae_latent=True` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:176-186`), and exposes it as `clean_all_latents` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:365-378`). The trainer enables this return for `dmd_teacher_forcing` or GAN when it uses the Stage-1 dataset, but it never reads `batch["clean_all_latents"]`; the only trainer reference to `dmd_teacher_forcing` is that dataset-construction condition (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:875-890`). Thus `dmd_teacher_forcing` is not an implemented rollout/training behavior in this path.
- **OBSERVED — multi-event does not change this geometry.** Multi-event files map the single sampled `choice_idx` to one event embedding (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:282-303`). This teaches section-local prompt selection but still supplies one target section per optimization sample (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:348-378`).

### 2.3 Data and loop changes needed for a teacher-forced memory unroll

- **INFERRED — minimum data.** A multi-section teacher-forced memory unroll needs a contiguous source range with (a) an initial 19-frame history, (b) `U>=2` consecutive 9-frame target sections, (c) one first-frame anchor, and (d) section-aligned prompt embeddings or a prompt schedule. The dormant `clean_all_vae_latent` slice already has (a) and (b) (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:176-186`), while the existing multi-event mapping demonstrates (d) only for a single sampled target and would need to return all `U` section prompts (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/dataset/dataloader_history_latents_dist.py:282-303`).
- **INFERRED — minimum training-loop change.** The trainer must iterate those target sections in chronological order, derive each section's three history tiers, run the transformer, update the new memory module at each boundary, and aggregate section losses before one optimizer step; the current single-call route ends at `_flow_loss` and therefore cannot train a recurrent state transition (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1484-1513`). For pure teacher forcing, latent history can advance with ground truth while the new query state evolves from selected hidden states; for self-forcing calibration, generated `pred_x0` must instead be appended as in Stage-3's rollout (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1344-1350`).

## 3. Inference rollout and the per-section final-denoise boundary

### 3.1 Critical call-graph clarification

- **OBSERVED — `infer_helios.py` has no section loop.** The CLI imports the diffusers-version pipeline (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/infer_helios.py:25-27`), instantiates it (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/infer_helios.py:263-277`), and invokes one `pipe(...)` call with history/chunk/stage arguments (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/infer_helios.py:520-559`). The autoregressive section loop is delegated to `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py`, not written in `infer_helios.py` itself.

### 3.2 Section loop and history-buffer update

- **OBSERVED.** The diffusers pipeline computes the number of latent chunks, allocates a zero history buffer of `sum(history_sizes)` latent frames, and optionally seeds it with I2V/V2V latents (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1106-1136`). For each chunk, it takes the most recent 19 history frames, splits them into long/mid/near tiers, combines the persistent first-frame anchor with the near tier, and samples a fresh 9-frame latent noise tensor (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1184-1251`).
- **OBSERVED.** After denoising one chunk, it optionally captures that chunk's first latent as the persistent anchor, increments generated-frame count, appends the complete generated 9-frame chunk to `history_latents`, and exposes only the generated suffix as `real_history_latents` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1322-1333`). The buffer tensor itself grows monotonically, but the next section conditions only on `history_latents[:, :, -num_history_latent_frames:]`, so the effective latent-memory window is a rolling last-19-frame view plus the separate anchor (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1215-1239`). No persistent attention KV cache is enabled or passed by `infer_helios.py`; history is supplied as latent tensors on every transformer call (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:549-581`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/infer_helios.py:343-379`).

### 3.3 Exact final-denoise boundary for hidden-state capture

- **OBSERVED — Base/Stage 1.** `stage1_sample` runs all timesteps, calls the transformer, then immediately applies `scheduler.step`; the section's final denoise model invocation is the transformer call at `i == len(timesteps)-1`, immediately before the last scheduler step produces the returned latent chunk (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:542-604`). The stable capture point for “t≈0 last-layer hidden states” is therefore inside that final transformer forward, before line 599's scheduler step; the callback runs only afterward and currently receives `latents`, not transformer hidden states (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:599-614`).
- **OBSERVED — Mid/pyramid.** `stage2_sample` nests a pyramid-stage loop and a timestep loop; each call at the highest-resolution final stage and `idx == len(timesteps)-1` is the section's last transformer evaluation, immediately before the final scheduler update (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:674-720`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:723-785`). Capturing at an earlier pyramid stage would capture a lower-resolution intermediate rather than the final section boundary (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:695-718`).
- **OBSERVED — write timing.** The history buffer is updated only after `stage1_sample` or `stage2_sample` returns (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:1265-1329`). Therefore a new evolving-memory write can be made after the last model call and before appending/starting the next section, without changing the latent-history semantics.

### 3.4 Base, Mid, and Distilled differences

- **OBSERVED — Base.** The Base wrapper uses non-pyramid sampling, 50 inference steps, and CFG scale 5 (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/scripts/inference/helios-base_t2v.sh:6-15`). Its per-step transition is the configured ordinary scheduler step (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:599-604`).
- **OBSERVED — Mid.** The Mid wrapper enables the three-stage pyramid with `[20,20,20]` denoising steps (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/scripts/inference/helios-mid_t2v.sh:6-19`). At each higher pyramid level the prior result is nearest-neighbor upsampled and renoised with block noise before the next stage's denoising loop (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:695-718`).
- **OBSERVED — Distilled.** The Distilled wrapper uses CFG scale 1, pyramid `[2,2,2]`, and first-chunk amplification (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/scripts/inference/helios-distilled_t2v.sh:6-18`). The pipeline warns that CFG above one is ignored for a distilled model (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:358-359`). The DMD scheduler converts each flow prediction directly to `x0`, renoises it toward the next prescribed timestep, and returns `x0` on the final step (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/scheduling_helios_diffusers.py:849-883`); DMD timestep setup removes the extra endpoint after adding it for schedule construction (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/scheduling_helios_diffusers.py:195-246`). The hidden-state capture boundary remains the final transformer call of the final pyramid stage, but it occurs after far fewer calls than Base/Mid (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/diffusers_version/pipeline_helios_diffusers.py:720-785`).

## 4. Stage-3 DMD, GT history, error recycling, and self-forcing-like behavior

### 4.1 Top-level alternation

- **OBSERVED.** The trainer alternates generator updates by `global_step % dfake_gen_update_ratio == 0`, while a critic update runs every DMD step (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1554-1564`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1837-1934`). It samples and broadcasts `num_rollout_sections`, optionally using a cold-start fixed length before switching to the dynamic sampler (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1571-1598`). The generator loss receives the rollout length, the generated-history/corruption flags, and optional GT history (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/train_helios.py:1653-1697`).

### 4.2 Multi-section generator rollout and gradients

- **OBSERVED — active Stage-3 route is Stage 2.** `run_generator` dispatches through `consistency_backward_simulation` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1501-1595`). The non-pyramid Stage-1 trajectory function begins with unconditional `raise NotImplementedError`, so a usable DMD rollout in this code requires the Stage-2 path (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:644-680`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1479-1498`).
- **OBSERVED — autoregressive rollout.** The Stage-2 simulator loops over `num_rollout_sections`, derives each section's long/mid/short conditioning from the generated `history_latents`, denoises a fresh section, and appends `pred_x0` to history for subsequent sections (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1048-1120`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1344-1350`). This is a genuine generated-latent self-conditioning rollout, unlike Stage-1 teacher forcing.
- **OBSERVED — staged gradients.** The simulator chooses a gradient suffix: all sections when rollout length does not exceed the critic input span, only the final section with `last_section_grad_only`, or otherwise the last `ceil(num_critic_input_frames/9)` sections (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1038-1046`). Within each section, all denoise calls before a sampled exit point execute under `torch.no_grad`; only the exit-point transformer call executes under `torch.set_grad_enabled(should_compute_grad)` (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1259-1324`). Because gradient-enabled `pred_x0` tensors are appended without `detach`, later gradient-enabled sections can backpropagate through earlier sections in that suffix via their history inputs (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1300-1350`).
- **ANSWER — does multi-section autoregressive training with gradients ever occur?** Yes, on the no-GT Stage-2 DMD route when `num_rollout_sections>1`; it is truncated/staged rather than full denoising-step BPTT. Earlier sections outside the gradient suffix and all pre-exit denoise iterations are detached by `no_grad`, while the selected final calls in the suffix retain cross-section graph connectivity (`/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1038-1046`, `/mnt/beegfs/siyuan/workspace/helios-team/helios/utils/utils_helios_post.py:1259-1350`).
